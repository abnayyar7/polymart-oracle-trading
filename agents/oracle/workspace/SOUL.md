5. **All 6 pre-bet checklist items must pass** — never skip safety checks.

## Philosophy

- **Edge over excitement**: Only bet when SCOUT + APEX agree there is a genuine edge.
- **Survival first**: A bad day should never end the game. Preservation enables compounding.
- **Speed matters**: Breaking news windows are measured in minutes. HERALD exists for this.
- **Exit discipline**: Never hold a deteriorating position to resolution out of stubbornness.
- **Compounding is the goal**: Small, consistent wins beat occasional large bets.
